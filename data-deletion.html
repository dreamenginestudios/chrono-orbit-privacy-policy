Data Deletion Request — Chrono Orbit

Developer: Dream Engine Studios

App: Chrono Orbit (package name: com.dreamengine.chronoorbit)

How to request deletion of your data

Send an email to dream.engine.studios.de@gmail.com with the subject line "Chrono Orbit — Data Deletion Request".

Since the app does not require a username or password to play, please include in your message:

  • Your in-game nickname (shown in your Profile) and/or the approximate date you started playing, so we can locate your account record.
  • If you signed in with Google, the email address of that Google account.
  • Optionally, a screenshot of your in-game Profile screen, which helps us confirm the record.

We will process your request and confirm deletion by email within 30 days.

What data will be deleted

  • Your account identifier (Firebase Authentication UID) and, if you signed in with Google or linked Discord, the link to those accounts
  • Your saved game progress stored on our servers (gold, crystals, keys, owned skins, upgrade levels, mission progress, leaderboard entries, clan membership)
  • Your chat messages stored on our server
  • Technical logs (game errors and diagnostics) linked to your identifier
  • Any nickname or avatar selection associated with your account

What may be retained

  • Aggregated, non-identifying data already processed by Google services (for example AdMob and its advertising partners) before the deletion request. We cannot delete data that those companies hold under their own policies.
  • Records of in-app purchases that Google Play itself retains for financial/legal/tax compliance purposes, independent of our app (governed by Google's own policies, not controlled by Dream Engine Studios).

Local data

Uninstalling the app from your device immediately deletes all locally stored save data. This page covers deletion of the copy of your data stored on our backend (Firebase), (Firebase and our own server), which uninstalling alone does not remove.

See also our Privacy Policy.
